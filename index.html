About

I build the software. You get your time back.

Hello there!

I'm Faith Mitchell.

For nine years I've worked at Haliburton Hockey Haven, a summer sports camp. I started as a counsellor and worked my way up to program director, a role I've held for the last five years.

Last year I started learning how to build websites and apps, and I decided to build something that would make my own job easier. The result was a complete scheduling app for camp. It ran for my last summer and will stay a part of camp going forward.

With it, I spent a third as much time in the office. That meant more time with the campers and staff, which has always been the most important part of camp for me.

Now I want to give other businesses the same thing: less time on the computer, and more time with the people.
